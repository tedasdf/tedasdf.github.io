## 1. Introduction
DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp introduces major innovations for long-context LLMs and large-scale RL. Key updates include **DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA)** and **Masked Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)** with unbiased KL divergence alignment. These mechanisms improve inference efficiency, training stability, and reasoning capabilities.

## 2. DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA)

### 2.1 Core Concept
DSA reduces attention complexity from $O(L^2)$ to $O(Lk)$, selecting the most relevant $k$ tokens using a **Light Indexer**. This transforms attention into a retrieval-style operation.

### 2.2 Light Indexer Mechanics
The Light Indexer determines the connectivity between query $t$ and key $s$ by projecting them into a lower-dimensional latent space.

**The Indexing Equation:**
$$I_{t,s} = \sum_{j=1}^{H} w_{t,j}^I \cdot \text{ReLU} (q_{t,j}^I \cdot k_s^I)$$

**Sparse Attention Computation:**
The output $u_t$ is computed only for tokens within the Top-k set:
$$u_t = \text{Attn}(h_t, \{c_s\}) \text{ where } I_{t,s} \in \text{Top-k}(I_{t,:})$$

### 2.3 Optimization & Loss ($L_I$)
To ensure the Light Indexer learns to mimic dense attention distributions, DeepSeek uses a dual-stage KL-divergence loss:

* **Warmup Stage (Dense):**
    $$L_I = \sum_{t} D_{KL} (p_{t,:} \parallel \text{Softmax}(I_{t,:}))$$
* **Sparse Stage (Fine-tuned):**
    $$L_I = \sum_{t} D_{KL} (p_{t, S_t} \parallel \text{Softmax}(I_{t, S_t}))$$
    *(Where $S_t$ represents the indices of the selected sparse tokens.)*

## 3. Masked GRPO and Unbiased KL

### 3.1 Motivation
Off-policy RL with large rollout batches introduces significant policy drift. Standard KL penalties or PPO clipping alone cannot stabilize updates at the scale DeepSeek-V3.2 operates.

### 3.2 The GRPO Objective
Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) eliminates the need for a separate critic model by normalizing rewards within a group ($G$) of outputs. 

**The Objective Function:**
$$J_{GRPO}(\theta) = E_{q \sim P(Q), \{o_i\}_{i=1}^G \sim \pi_{\theta_{old}}} \left[ \frac{1}{G} \sum_{i=1}^G \left( \min \left( \frac{\pi_\theta(o_i|q)}{\pi_{\theta_{old}}(o_i|q)} A_i, \text{clip} \left( \frac{\pi_\theta(o_i|q)}{\pi_{\theta_{old}}(o_i|q)}, 1-\epsilon, 1+\epsilon \right) A_i \right) - \beta D_{KL}(\pi_\theta \parallel \pi_{ref}) \right) \right]$$

### 3.3 Hard Masking
Sequences with negative advantage and KL above a threshold $\delta$ are zeroed out. This creates a multi-layer trust region that enables massive off-policy batch reuse without catastrophic drift.

## 4. Interaction & Research Insights

* **System Synergy:** DSA enables long-context RL rollouts by lowering memory overhead, while Masked GRPO stabilizes the resulting heavy off-policy updates.
* **Discrete Routing:** Sparse routing is non-differentiable; dense warmup is essential to ensure meaningful gradient flow before hard sparsity is applied.
* **Residual Stability:** Residual stream statistics must adapt to sparse connectivity to avoid fragmented activations.
* **Kernel Fusion:** Specialized Triton or sparse CUDA kernels are required to prevent IO latency from negating the computational gains of $O(Lk)$ complexity.

> **Summary:** The combination of learned sparse indexing and masked group-relative updates allows DeepSeek-V3.2 to sustain a post-training compute budget exceeding 10% of total pretraining costs.
